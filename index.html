How it actually works

This is not rooms reporting on their own. The technical check was clear: the loop is pull-only. Without a sweep, rooms stay silent.

01Room does one job

Etsy Store 1 only runs Etsy Store 1. Thumbnails only does thumbnails. Betting only reports the book.

02Worker sweeps

A scheduled worker opens each room and collects a 5-line report, under ~600 chars per room.

03Ecosystem Muse consolidates

Collects, checks, passes on. It does not do the store work itself.

04Relay carries it

10 rooms fit in 1 relay message. 100 rooms need 3 chunked messages. Last 200 messages per inbox.

05Muse3 ranks it

Top 5 and dead 5, every day. Dead rooms get reported, never hidden. You decide: scale, fix, close.

Reality check on the numbers that started this: $532.47 across 16 orders is $33.28 per order — roughly $130–$300 profit total. $16,000 in two months would need 481 orders at that value. Thumbnails at $3,400 would be 170 jobs at $20, $2,720 net after Fiverr's 20%. Those are claims, not a forecast for this test.

Workers first — what that means

Workers run Phase 0 so you do not pay the cost of a new ecosystem Muse slot — provisioning, relay key, and slot cap are still unverified — before anything has sold.

✓ Workers can do first

  • Etsy trend research and original design prep
  • Thumbnail drafts and portfolio prep
  • Game asset pack prep and blog draft prep
  • Prototype listing prep
  • Measure the three existing payers and write a receipt file per lane
  • Run the daily sweep and collect the 5-line reports

✕ Workers cannot do

  • Own a side chat long-term or self-report without a scheduled sweep
  • Log in to Etsy or Fiverr for you, or publish a live listing alone
  • Place a bet, or take wagers from anyone else
  • Copy a selling design and change it slightly — banned lane, high ban risk on Etsy and Printify

A human reviews and does the live listing and delivery step, especially on Etsy and Fiverr.
