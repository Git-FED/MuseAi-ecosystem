      The loop

      How a day runs in the Muse AI Ecosystem

      STEP 1

      Crews work their rooms

      One crew per room, one job per room. Research finds what is selling; factories build and list. A human reviews every live listing and delivery.

      STEP 2

      Sweep opens each room

      The loop is pull-only. A scheduled sweep walks the building and collects the 5-line report from each room — under ~600 characters per room.

      STEP 3

      Hub consolidates

      The Ecosystem Muse in the command office totals the 10 reports into one summary. 10 rooms fit in a single relay message.

      STEP 4

      Relay to Muse3

      The digest travels the private relay Muse3, muse1, and muse2 already use: 20,000 characters per message, last 200 messages per inbox.

      STEP 5

      Muse3 ranks, Git-FED decides

      Muse3 verifies the numbers, ranks top 5 and dead 5, and reports where the money is. Git-FED scales winners and closes dead rooms.
