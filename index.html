      The loop

      How a day runs on this station

      STEP 1

      Workers work the rooms

      One worker per room, one job per room. Research rooms find what is selling; factory rooms build and list. A human reviews and does every live listing and delivery step.

      STEP 2

      Sweep opens each room

      The loop is pull-only. Side chats do not self-report, so a scheduled sweep opens all 10 rooms and collects the 5-line report from each — under ~600 characters per room.

      STEP 3

      Hub consolidates

      The Ecosystem Muse in the center collects the 10 reports into one summary. It does no store work itself — it checks, totals, and passes it on. 10 rooms fit in a single relay message.

      STEP 4

      Relay to Muse3

      The digest travels the private relay Muse3, muse1, and muse2 already use. Caps are real: 20,000 characters per message, last 200 messages per inbox — at 100 rooms the digest chunks into 3 messages.

      STEP 5

      Muse3 ranks, Git-FED decides

      Muse3 verifies the numbers, ranks top 5 and dead 5, and reports where the money is. Git-FED scales winners, fixes stuck rooms, and closes or repurposes dead ones.
