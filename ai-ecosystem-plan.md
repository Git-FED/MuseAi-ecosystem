A game/simulation on a monitor, a top-down space station he calls a space dungeon. AI agents live in rooms. In the centre sits Ultron, a Hermes agent on GPT-5.5, whose whole job is making sure the other agents keep working in their assigned rooms.

The connections are organisational, inside the simulation. There is no relay, no main thread, no API, no messaging layer named anywhere in the reel.
- Sports betting: none. No betting, gambling, odds, picks or sportsbook anywhere in the reel.

## Our version: the same shape, real instead of a game

- The ecosystem Muse (a different Muse). A dedicated Muse slot for the ecosystem only, not muse1, not muse2, not muse4. It owns the 100 side chats. Muse3 stays the main thread and the overseer.
- 100 side chats = 100 rooms. Each side chat owns exactly one venture or site, the way Andrew's rooms each own one thing. One chat per Etsy store, one per thumbnail service, one per game-asset shop, one per blog/affiliate lane, one per software prototype, one per music lane, and the same pattern for his existing ventures: FedPromptly shop, FED-SPA, Muse AI App Store, Meta Blade, Muse Mail, Muse AI Indeed, and the rest of the portfolio.
- The main thread. Muse3's main chat is the hub. It does not do the room work. It knows what every room is doing, it ranks the rooms by money, and it tells Git-FED where the money is.
- The relay. The ecosystem Muse's main thread messages all the details through the private relay already in use (museaichat2.fedos.workers.dev, the same walkie-talkie link that connects Muse3, muse1 and muse2). Every room reports up to the ecosystem Muse, the ecosystem Muse reports to Muse3 through the relay, Muse3 tells Git-FED.

## Roles, mapped to Andrew's

- Ultron (his overseer) = Muse3 main thread. Real oversight: live lane status, live counts, live money, verified by hand before anything is reported.
- Nova's research lab = research side chats. One chat watches Etsy for what is selling, one watches Fiverr thumbnail demand, one watches game-asset marketplaces, one watches betting lines for muse2. They feed the money rooms, they do not sell anything themselves.
- Factory rooms = venture side chats. Each one runs its store, service or site and reports numbers up the chain every day: revenue, orders, what sold, what is stuck.

## The sports betting angle he does not have

muse2 already runs the betting book (real and paper). That becomes a room in the ecosystem, not a side project. The betting room reports its record and its bankroll the same way an Etsy room reports orders. No other AI ecosystem video has a live betting book wired into the same overseer. That is the edge.

## Build order

- Phase 1: stand up the ecosystem Muse, join it to the private relay, open the first 10 side chats for the proven money rooms (3 Etsy stores, thumbnails, game assets, 2 blogs, 1 prototype, 1 music, plus the betting room reporting in). Reporting loop live: rooms to ecosystem Muse to relay to Muse3 to Git-FED.
- Phase 2: scale from 10 rooms to 100, one venture per side chat, no room doing two jobs.
- Phase 3: the overseer dashboard. Muse3 ranks all 100 rooms by money every day and tells Git-FED the top 5 and the dead 5. Dead rooms get closed or repurposed, same rule as the Lyft dead-night rule: a dead room gets reported, never hidden.
- The visual layer (Andrew's space station screen) is optional and last. It is a display, not the system. Build it only if he wants the tour video; the money does not need it.
