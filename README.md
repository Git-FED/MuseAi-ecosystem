## Realistic plan

See `ai-ecosystem-realistic-plan.md` for the full realistic plan, and `ai-ecosystem-plan.md` for the original plan it replaces.

Key points, verified Nov 25 2026:
- Structure can work with changes. Reporting is pull-only, not push. A scheduled sweep opens each room and collects a capped 5-line report.
- Relay caps: 20,000 chars per message, last 200 messages per inbox, no broadcast.
- Etsy copying method is out. Original designs only, with Printify and AI disclosures.
- Money: realistic first 90 days across the first 10 rooms is $0 to $1,000 total profit, most likely low hundreds. Andrew's $16,000 Etsy and $3,400 thumbnails are claims, not a forecast.
- Betting is reporting only. Real book Nov 24: 30-14, -$5.37. Count $0 income.
